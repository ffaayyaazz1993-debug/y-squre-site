Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

In plain terms: this site has no accounts, no login, no contact form and no analytics of our own. If you are not emailing us, the only information involved is ordinary web-server log data that the hosting provider records so the site can be delivered and secured. Advertising cookies are set by Google AdSense and are described below.

Y-Square is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser. Nothing on this site is investment advice, an offer, or a solicitation to buy or sell any security.

What we do not collect

  • No account or registration. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No contact form, so no form submissions or stored enquiry records.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or profiling tools of our own.
  • No third-party embeds, social widgets, or map services that would transmit your visit to another platform.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information.

Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies to you, we rely on the following bases:

  • Legitimate interests — responding to enquiries you sent us, and securing and operating the site.
  • Consent — setting personalised advertising cookies, which you can withdraw at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.
  • Legal obligation — retaining records where required by law.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS with a valid certificate, so data in transit between you and the site is encrypted. Administrative access to the hosting account is restricted, and the account uses two-factor authentication.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We do our part; we cannot promise absolute security.

Data sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers who host the site and deliver it to you, strictly on our instructions. We may disclose information if legally required to do so.

Data retention

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as needed to deal with the matter. We do not keep marketing lists — we do not have one. Server log retention follows the hosting provider's defaults.

Your rights

Where the GDPR or a comparable law applies to you, you have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete data we no longer have a reason to keep;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing;
  • withdraw consent, at any time, without giving a reason;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

In practice: we hold very little, so most requests are simple to satisfy. Email us and we will deal with it.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Third-party links

Our content may reference central banks, statistical agencies and news sources. Those sites are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one. We are not responsible for their practices.

Changes to this policy
