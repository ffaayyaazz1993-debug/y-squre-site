## A. Contextual Summary (Source-Accurate)

Eleven people were taken to hospital with injuries after a bar collapsed in San Vicente de la Sonsierra in La Rioja, northern Spain. The collapse occurred around 5:25 a.m. (0325 GMT), trapping between 25 and 30 people, according to the Spanish government in La Rioja. Emergency services rescued people from the rubble, and firefighters checked whether anyone remained trapped. Survivors said the floor of the bar collapsed, followed by the ceiling. Police have launched an investigation into the cause.
