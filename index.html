About Y-Square The practice

Y-Square is an independent investment research practice. We believe markets are driven by the intersection of macroeconomic cycles, policy shifts and geopolitical events — and that understanding these forces is the edge.

Our research combines primary sources — central bank publications, government data, and original analysis — with disciplined valuation work. We tell you what the data says, not what sells headlines.

What we stand for

  • Primary-source research, always cited
  • Scenario analysis over single-point forecasts
  • Position sizing and risk before return chasing
  • Plain-language communication
